Professional Summary

With over 10 years of experience as a Java Full Stack Developer, I specialize in designing, developing, and deploying robust, scalable web and cloud-based applications. My technical expertise includes Java/J2EE, Spring Boot, Hibernate, Microservices architecture, and modern front-end frameworks such as Angular and React. I have a strong track record of delivering high-quality software solutions across industries like banking, airlines, and travel, while actively following Agile/SCRUM methodologies. I consistently apply best practices in the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and embrace Test-Driven Development (TDD) to ensure reliability, performance, and maintainability of code. I’m passionate about building end-to-end systems that are both efficient and user-centric..

I hold certifications as an AWS Certified Developer – Associate, Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA), and Certified Java Developer from Coding Shuttle. These credentials validate my expertise in building and deploying cloud-native applications, orchestrating containerized workloads, and crafting robust Java-based solutions. With this strong foundation, I consistently deliver scalable, efficient, and secure software systems that address complex business needs and accelerate digital transformation initiatives.
